3 Flagship Drive Systems

MRL, Traction & Hydraulic Elevator Systems

We engineer and install three core vertical drive architectures—each tailored to your building's headroom, floor count, and passenger or freight duty cycle.

01 • Space-Saving

MRL (Machine Room-Less)

Compact gearless drive mounted inside the hoistway shaft—eliminating rooftop machine rooms and reducing power consumption.

  • No overhead machine room needed
  • Quiet, energy-saving gearless traction
  • Ideal for apartments & commercial buildings
View MRL Benefits →
02 • High-Duty

Traction Elevators

Balanced steel-rope and counterweight traction drive built for continuous daily passenger, hospital, and goods traffic.

  • Balanced counterweight energy efficiency
  • Smooth multi-floor travel speeds
  • Proven durability for high-traffic buildings
View Traction Benefits →
03 • Villa & Low-Rise

Hydraulic Elevators

Fluid-driven piston lift systems requiring minimal overhead clearance and pit depth for private homes, villas, and heavy loads.

  • Minimal pit depth & overhead clearance
  • Whisper-quiet start & stop ride comfort
  • Zero structural load on rooftop beams
